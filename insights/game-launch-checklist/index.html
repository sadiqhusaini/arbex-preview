Your game is playable. Is it ready to launch?

A practical checklist beyond the final build.

By ARBEX Editorial · Sample editorial guide

Educational sample content. This article does not describe verified ARBEX client experience.

A release candidate needs testing on real target devices, including slow networks and interrupted sessions.

Review store assets, age ratings, privacy disclosures, consent flows, SDK behavior, and purchase restoration. Requirements vary by store and region; verify them before submission.

Define events before integrating analytics. Check that consent and data collection match your published disclosures.

Plan a staged release, rollback process, crash monitoring, and a support channel. Publishing is the beginning of maintenance.
