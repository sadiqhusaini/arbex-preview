USEFUL TO KNOW

Your questions, considered.

Can you help with one specific task?

Yes. Tell us what you already have and where you need help. We can scope a focused engagement or discuss a complete project.

How are budget and timeline agreed?

After reviewing your requirements and dependencies, we agree on scope, delivery expectations, and a proposal. No fixed timeline or price is assumed before discovery.

Can you work with an existing product?

We begin with an assessment of the current implementation, access requirements, and known issues before proposing changes.

What happens after delivery?

Support and maintenance are agreed as part of your engagement. Documentation and handover expectations are discussed before work begins.
